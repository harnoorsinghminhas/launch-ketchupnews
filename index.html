Superintelligence (SI)

The new name for the next generation of artificial intelligence. A name, not a claim that machines now outthink people.

Artificial intelligence (AI)

Software that does things we used to need human judgement for, like writing, summarizing or spotting patterns.

Agent

An AI helper that carries out a task for you, step by step, instead of only answering a question.

Hallucination

When an AI states something confidently that isn't true. It's why every card here links a source.
